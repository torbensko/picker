Picker 1.0.0
===========

- Find similar photos directly in your Apple Photos library, with on-device keeper recommendations.
- Compare large previews, keep multiple photos, and review every removal before deleting.
- Apply choices to one group or review all pending photos together.
- Export screenshots to a verified archive before removing them from Photos.
- Reuse saved analysis and review decisions. Scanning never downloads iCloud photos.
- Check for signed app updates from the Picker menu.

Requires macOS 26 or later.
